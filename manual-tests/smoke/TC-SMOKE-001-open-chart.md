# Preconditions

- A tester can sign in to staging.
- At least one chart exists in the account.

| Step | Action | Expected result | Desktop | iPad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open the chart list. | An existing chart is visible. | Required | Required |
| 2 | Open the chart. | The editor and chart canvas load without an error. | Required | Required |
| 3 | Return to the chart list. | The chart remains available. | Required | Required |
